On the oval:

  1. Stand in the middle of the oval and tap Walk the battlefield.
  2. Point the phone at the grass and sweep it slowly until the battle appears around you, life size. The zombies are about twice your height, and the MK II towers like a three-storey building.
  3. Walk! The death rays run towards the castle end; the gravestone where the zombies rise is at the other end.

Best on a bright day with the grass in view. Walk at a steady pace and keep the phone up so it keeps its place. If the battle drifts or jumps, close it and place it again. And keep an eye out for real goalposts and people while you're looking through the phone.

In the lounge room: tap View in your room, point at the floor and move the phone slowly until the battle appears. Lean right in to see the troops, missiles and explosions up close.

This screen can't do AR. Open this page on an iPhone, iPad or Android phone. You can still spin and zoom the battle here.
