On the oval:

  1. Stand in the centre of the oval, facing along its length, and tap Walk around it life size.
  2. Point the phone at the grass and sweep it slowly until the rocket appears. It's 110 m long, so most of it is behind and beyond you: turn around.
  3. Walk to the engines first: five of them, each as wide as a car is long. Then walk the length to the nose, and look up at the standing one.

An AFL oval is about 135–185 m long, so it fits. Plain grass gives the phone little to lock onto: a bright day, goalposts or fences in view, a steady walk and the phone held up all help. If it drifts or jumps, close it and place it again. Watch for real people and posts while you look through the screen.

In the lounge room: tap View in your room and point at the floor. At 1:100 the rocket is 1.1 m tall and a 1.8 m person would be 18 mm. A good way to check the phone works before going to the oval.
