Tower Clash

Two players. One sends the enemies, one builds the towers.

How to play
  • Defender: tap a tower design, then tap an empty grass square to build it. Tap + New tower to invent your own: name, colour, damage, range, speed and a special power. Better towers cost more gold. Tap a built tower to sell it.
  • Attacker: spend energy to send enemies down the path. Energy refills faster as the game goes on. Mix fast, tough and healing enemies to break through.
  • The attacker wins if the defender's lives reach 0. The defender wins by surviving for 4 minutes.

Online play needs both devices on the internet. To play with no internet, use “Both players on this device”.
