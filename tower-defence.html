Tower Defence

Two players. One sends the enemies, one builds the towers.

How to play
  • Start: tap Play with a friend, pick your side, then tap Send invite and text it. When your friend taps the link, the game starts. Friend sitting next to you? They can scan the QR code with their camera.
  • 🛡 Tower builder: tap a tower, then tap the grass to build it. Tap + New tower to invent your own: name, colour, damage, range, speed and a special power. Better towers cost more gold. Tap a built tower to sell it.
  • 👾 Enemy sender: spend energy to send enemies. HIDDEN Shadows can only be shot by 📡 sensor towers. FLYING Skull Drones skip the road. SHIELD soaks hits until it breaks. The BOSS Mega Mech fires EMPs that stun towers and rages at half health.
  • Enemies win if the base's lives reach 0. Towers win by lasting 4 minutes. Then swap sides!

Playing with a friend needs internet or mobile data on both phones. No internet? Use Two players, one phone.
