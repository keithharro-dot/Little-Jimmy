How to play
  • Start: tap Play with a friend, pick your side, then tap Send invite and text it. When your friend taps the link, the game starts. Friend sitting next to you? They can scan the QR code with their camera.
  • 🛡 Tower builder: tap a tower, then tap the grass to build it. Tap + New tower to invent your own: name, colour, damage, range, speed and a special power. Better towers cost more gold. You earn more gold as the zombie sender's eco grows. Tap a built tower to see its range, upgrade it (up to level 4) or sell it. 💚 Reviver brings back destroyed towers; 🎧 DJ boosts towers near it.
  • 🧟 Zombie sender: spend energy to send zombies. You start earning ⚡5 a second, and every zombie you send adds eco to raise that for the rest of the game (bigger zombies add more). Each zombie has a build time before you can send another of that type. Eradicators cost eco, so save them for the right moment. Eco tops out at ⚡150 a second. HIDDEN Phantoms can only be shot by 📡 sensor towers. FLYING Zombie Bats skip the road. SHIELD soaks hits until it breaks. The BOSS Eradicators fire rocket bursts that knock out towers (a tower stunned 3 times is destroyed), and the MK II rages at half health.
  • Zombies win if the base's 150 health reaches 0. Towers win by surviving all 50 waves. Waves come automatically, with an Eradicator on wave 20, an MK II on wave 40 and the Void on wave 50. Then swap sides!

Playing with a friend needs internet or mobile data on both phones. No internet? Use Two players, one phone.
