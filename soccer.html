How to play

Drag your players (the ones with a glow) with your finger. Bump the ball to pass, run into it fast to shoot. Keepers stay in their box; nobody else can go in a box. Score in the other team's goal!

Playing together: everyone must be in the same place on the same Wi-Fi (or one phone's hotspot). The host taps Host a match, friends scan the QR code. With more players, teams are split: e.g. 4 players = defence and strikers on each side.
