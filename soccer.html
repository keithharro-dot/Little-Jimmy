How to play

Drag your players (the ones with a glow) with your finger. Bump the ball to pass, run into it fast to shoot. Keepers stay in their box; nobody else can go in a box. Score in the other team's goal!

Playing together: everyone must be in the same place on the same Wi-Fi (or one phone's hotspot). The host taps Host a match, and friends scan the QR code on the host's screen with their camera. The QR code changes every 30 seconds, so you have to be there to scan it. Each side gets one player per person (1 v 1 = one player each), plus a keeper: the computer plays the keeper until someone on that team grabs it, then whoever grabbed it controls it. 4 players = defence and striker on each side.

⚡ Chaos power-ups: every time a team lets in a goal, it gets a new power — turbo boots, banana peels, the human cannon, tennis rackets, the ghost curse, a freeze ray, magnet boots, a brick wall, a homing rocket ball, and finally the NUCLEAR BALL. In the last 45 seconds (or at match point) it's the BOSS ROUND: one player on each team turns into a giant boss with the boss name they chose. Powers stack, so the longer the match goes, the crazier it gets. Prefer a plain game? Pick Normal soccer.
