How to play

The joystick at the bottom left steers your player nearest the ball. Your powers sit in a row above it: tap one to fire it (bananas, the freeze ray and the ghost curse go off at once; the cannon, rocket and nuke arm, then go off when you next touch the ball). Two quick taps on the stick fire the last power you picked. Or touch anywhere on the pitch and drag: the nearest of your players (the ones with a glow) follows your finger. Best is to put your finger a little below the player, so you can see it; if you land right on it, it slides out from under your finger as you move. A dashed line shows which player you're holding. Bump the ball to pass, run into it fast to shoot. Keepers stay in their box; nobody else can go in a box. Score in the other team's goal!

Playing together: tap Invite friends to play and send the link (WhatsApp, Messages, anything). Friends next to you can just scan the QR code on your screen with their camera instead. Either way they tap Join game and they're in. The link works until the host leaves the lobby, so only send it to people you want in.

Each side gets one player per person (1 v 1 = one player each), plus a keeper: the computer plays the keeper until someone on that team grabs it, then whoever grabbed it controls it. 4 players = defence and striker on each side.

⚡ Chaos power-ups: every time a team lets in a goal, it gets a new power — turbo boots, banana peels, the human cannon, tennis rackets, the ghost curse, a freeze ray, magnet boots, a brick wall, a homing rocket ball, and finally the NUCLEAR BALL. In the last 45 seconds (or at match point) it's the BOSS ROUND: one player on each team turns into a giant boss with the boss name they chose. The ball becomes the ☢ LEO BOMB: touch it first to arm it, then hit anyone on the other team with it and their whole team is blasted across the pitch and frozen. Powers stack, so the longer the match goes, the crazier it gets.
